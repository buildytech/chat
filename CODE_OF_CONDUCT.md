# Code of Conduct

BuildY Chat welcomes people across backgrounds and experience levels. These rules apply to project Issues,
Discussions, other project-managed spaces, and representation of the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully; critique ideas and behavior rather than personal characteristics.
- Give constructive feedback and room to learn or correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent and reasonable requests to stop an interaction.
- Keep participation relevant; avoid spam, duplicate reports and bulk generated submissions.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, unauthorized publication of private information,
repeated disruption and retaliation against someone reporting a concern are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer at **alexyos.dev@gmail.com**, with links, context and privacy
concerns. Do not post private evidence in public threads. This private reporting channel's address is published so
participants can find it.

The maintainer reviews reports, seeks relevant context and limits sharing of identifying information to what is needed
to handle the concern. Absolute confidentiality and fixed response times cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may request a correction, remove content, warn participants,
temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban an account from project spaces. Serious cases may require
immediate restrictions. Explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information. Requests to reconsider
a decision may be sent to the same contact with new context.

Software vulnerabilities use the separate [security process](SECURITY.md).
