# Support and feedback

BuildY Chat is in development; the future hosted chat at `chat.buildy.tw` is not ready for public use.
[BuildY](https://buildy.tw) is the project website; components are available at [UI8Kit](https://ui.buildy.tw).

- Questions and early ideas: [Discussions](https://github.com/buildytech/chat/discussions).
- Reproducible bugs, concrete feature/UX proposals and documentation: [Issue forms](https://github.com/buildytech/chat/issues/new/choose).
- Vulnerabilities: [private reporting](SECURITY.md).
- Conduct/moderation concerns: the private contact in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Read the [README](README.md), [runbook](docs/runbook.md) and [Contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) first.
Support is best effort, without guaranteed response times. PRs and unsolicited patches are not accepted at this stage.
Private email is for sensitive reports, not general support.

Include commit/version, browser/OS, steps and expected/actual results. For UI problems include viewport, zoom, theme
and pane state. Sanitize logs and screenshots; never share keys, passwords or private session data.
